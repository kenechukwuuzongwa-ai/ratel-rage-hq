Prelaunch use notes for the site and its development materials. Final public terms require studio review. Last updated 17 September 2026.

Development status

Ratel Rage is in development. Prototype features, content, performance, and availability may change. The Android debug package is not a public release.

Your submissions

Send feedback, bug reports, and ideas you have the right to share. Do not upload someone else’s private information, media you do not control, or harmful content. Suggestions are not promises of future features. Those submission routes are part of the full application build and are not active on this static preview.

Site materials

The logo, character art, screenshots, copy, and game are Ratel Rage materials. Creator kit assets are provided to help cover or discuss the game with accurate development status. Do not imply an official partnership or alter assets in a misleading way.

Availability

The site and test services may be unavailable while the project changes. Public ideas may be moderated to remove spam or abuse.
